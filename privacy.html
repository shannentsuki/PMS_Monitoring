Privacy Policy

PMS Monitoring · Last updated: October 2026

PMS Monitoring is a private internal tool used to track preventive maintenance schedules. It is available only to people approved by the app administrator.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, the app receives your Google account's name and email address. It does not receive your password and does not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts, or any other Google data.

While you use the app, it records maintenance information you enter (schedules, completed tasks, findings, costs) and a log of changes you make (what was changed and when), together with your name and email.

How we use it

Your email is used only to confirm you are on the approved access list, to decide your role (Viewer, Editor or Admin), and to show who made each change.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in a Google Sheet owned by the app administrator and handled by Google Apps Script. It is not sold, shared with advertisers, or given to any third party.

Retention and removal

Access records and the change log are kept for as long as the app is in use. To be removed or to ask about your data, contact the app administrator.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date above shows the latest version.
